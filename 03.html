Typing is Fishing

Controls

Spacebar: start fishing / Letters: catch the fish

1: Change your color / 2: Reset your color / 9: Game Credits (note: this will reset your score!)

How to Play

Your little character is sitting and fishing. When they get surprised with the "!", that means you can press spacebar to start catching the fish. Type the word you see to catch the fish. If you do not feel like you can catch the fish, you can let it go by.
